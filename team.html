Corporate Leadership

Trey Jarrard

Chief Executive Officer

Trey co-founded Renewvia Energy in 2008 and has grown it into a developer, owner and operator of more than 100 MW of solar across the United States, Pacific Islands, South America and sub-Saharan Africa. He has led Renewvia Solar Africa since 2017. Earlier, he was a partner at The Convex Group and an executive at WebMD.

Debo Dosunmu

Managing Director West Africa

Debo leads Renewvia's West African business and directs engineering across the company's African portfolio. He joined Renewvia in 2021 as an electrical engineer, rose to Lead Engineer, and returned in 2024 after project development work at CrossBoundary. He previously led engineering teams at Pirano Energy and Daystar Power.

Douglas Cox

Managing Director East Africa

Douglas joined Renewvia in 2017 and has led its project development across Africa ever since, delivering solar minigrids and commercial and institutional systems with a focus on East Africa, including the Kakuma and Kalobeyei minigrids. A Georgia Tech-trained engineer, he previously designed solar products with SunFarmer in Nepal.
